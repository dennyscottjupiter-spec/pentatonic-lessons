QUICK BRIEFING

  • Pick a KEY (any of 12), then flip MINOR/MAJOR to hear the same shapes from a different root.
  • A minor lens: root = A. Sounds sad / bluesy / rock.
  • C major lens: root = C. Same shapes, sounds bright / poppy / country.
  • 5 box positions cover the whole neck. Press 1–5 on your keyboard to switch.
  • Try this: jam Box 1 in Am over a backing track, then hit ▶ START GAME to level up!

🎸 HOW TO USE

  • Pick a KEY from the 12 buttons — shapes shift to that key on the neck.
  • Toggle MINOR / MAJOR to move the highlighted root (the 5 notes stay the same).
  • Select a BOX POSITION (1–5) to focus on one region of the neck.
  • Click any fret to hear it. ROOT notes glow pink, SCALE NOTES glow cyan.

🏋️ PRACTICE LAB

⚡ DAILY DRILLS

  • Ascend + descend Box 1 slowly to a metronome click (start at 60 BPM)
  • Play every note in the box, naming it out loud as you go
  • Target the root note on beat 1 — let every phrase land there
  • Connect Box 1 → Box 2 without stopping (overlap frets 7-8)
  • 3-notes-per-string runs across all 6 strings, up and down
  • Practice "call and response" — play a 2-bar phrase, leave 2 bars silence
